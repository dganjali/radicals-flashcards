# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mandarin learners — primarily students working toward HSK or academic coursework, but useful to anyone who wants to build reading fluency from the ground up. They study in focused sessions, often late at night or between classes, and want an environment that respects the depth of what they're learning. The tool is built to be shared publicly.

## Product Purpose

A focused study tool for the 65 most common Mandarin radicals. Three modes — flashcards with spaced-repetition scoring, a multiple-choice quiz, and a browse grid — let learners approach the same material from different angles. Progress is saved locally. Success looks like a learner who can recognize radicals on sight and understand how they compose more complex characters.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, minimal, focused. The interface is a study environment, not a game. It should feel like a well-designed desk: everything in its place, nothing competing for attention. The Chinese script is the subject and the hero; the chrome should recede around it.

## Anti-references

- Duolingo and gamified language apps: streaks, XP bars, bouncy animations, cartoon mascots
- Generic SaaS dashboards: sidebar nav, data tables, blue-on-white, corporate utility feel
- Orientalist or touristy aesthetics: red lanterns, chopstick motifs, stereotyped "Chinese restaurant" visual language

## Design Principles

1. **Characters first** — the radical glyph is always the focal point of any screen. Interface elements earn space by serving the character, not competing with it.
2. **Focused depth** — one task per screen. No dashboard sprawl, no feature creep visible at rest. Complexity lives in the interactions, not the layout.
3. **Earned restraint** — resist the urge to celebrate or gamify. The subject has inherent difficulty and dignity; the UI respects that without being cold.
4. **Deliberate motion** — transitions are purposeful and unhurried. Nothing bounces. Motion signals state change, not personality.
5. **Ambient immersion** — dark environment, gold accent, classical type. The aesthetic should feel like studying by lamplight, not opening a productivity app.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Best effort: keyboard-navigable, readable contrast, screen-reader-friendly labels on interactive elements. No formal WCAG audit target.
